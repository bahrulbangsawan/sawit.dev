Database logos for the database sidebar, its add menu, the SQL editor's
header and the welcome screen, used to identify each kind of database
(nominative use; trademarks belong to their owners). The kind's name always
comes with the logo, in the tooltip and the accessible name.

- postgresql-color.svg: the PostgreSQL 3-colour elephant ("Slonik"), unmodified,
  from https://wiki.postgresql.org/wiki/Logo
  (https://wiki.postgresql.org/images/a/a4/PostgreSQL_logo.3colors.svg).
  Use follows the PostgreSQL Trademark Policy
  (https://www.postgresql.org/about/policies/trademarks/): fair use to refer to
  the software, the mark not modified. Postgres, PostgreSQL and the Slonik Logo
  are trademarks or registered trademarks of the PostgreSQL Community
  Association of Canada, and used with their permission.
- d1.svg: Cloudflare D1's product icon from Cloudflare's developer docs
  (https://github.com/cloudflare/cloudflare-docs, src/icons/d1.svg at
  30b3bfd33f95c201438befdd531a91193e358272), content licensed CC BY 4.0 by
  Cloudflare, Inc. One colour, drawn in Cloudflare orange the way the docs draw
  it in their text colour; the paths are unchanged. Cloudflare and D1 are
  trademarks of Cloudflare, Inc. The "Cloudflare D1" group header uses
  ../agent_logos/cloudflare-color.svg (LobeHub Icons, MIT; see that folder's
  SOURCES.md).
- sqlite-color.svg: the feather mark of SQLite's own logo,
  https://www.sqlite.org/images/sqlite370_banner.svg, with the "SQLite"
  wordmark paths and the white background left out and the viewBox cropped to
  the mark; no path is changed. SQLite is in the public domain
  (https://www.sqlite.org/copyright.html); the name is a trademark of Hipp,
  Wyrick & Company, Inc.

MySQL, MariaDB, Neon and Supabase have no logo here: Sawit doesn't detect those
databases as kinds of their own (Neon and Supabase connect as PostgreSQL).
